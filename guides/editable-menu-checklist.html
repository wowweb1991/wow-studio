Editable Restaurant Menu: A Checklist Before Printing

Prepare food names, prices, categories, branding and print settings before customizing an editable restaurant menu.

Gather the menu information

List each food or drink name, a short description and its current price. Group similar items into sections such as meals, snacks and drinks. Double-check serving sizes and any optional extras.

Make prices easy to read

Use consistent currency labels and align prices clearly. Check the menu on a phone and on a printed test sheet. Avoid shrinking text to fit more items; an extra page can be easier for customers to use.

Prepare your branding

Have your logo, business name and contact details ready. Choose a design with enough contrast for readable text. If dietary or certification claims appear on your menu, confirm that they accurately describe your business and products.

Print a proof

Confirm the page size, export settings and margins with your printer. Print one test copy and check spelling, prices and contact information before ordering a full batch.
