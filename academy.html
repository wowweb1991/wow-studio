Free Lesson 1: Introduction to AI

Objective: Understand what AI is, what it can do, and why human review matters.

Artificial intelligence can recognize patterns, generate text and images, and help analyze information. ChatGPT and Claude assist with writing and coding; Gemini assists with research and multimodal tasks; Canva offers design tools.

AI sometimes makes errors. Verify facts, review all client work, and avoid sharing confidential information without permission and safeguards.

Practice prompt

Act as a business mentor in the Philippines. I have ₱1,000 and want to start a small online business. Recommend three beginner-friendly business ideas, explain the startup expenses, identify potential customers, and give me a seven-day action plan.

Assignment: Choose one business idea, identify customers, estimate startup costs, and explain how AI can help.
